CDN Information

A Content Delivery Network (CDN) stores and delivers website content through servers located in different geographic locations.

When a user requests content that is already stored at an edge server, the CDN can provide a cache hit without requesting the content again from the origin server.

If the content is not available at the edge server, the CDN performs an origin fetch to obtain the content from the original server.
